Privacy Policy

RoomwiseTools Pin Scheduler (the "app"). Last updated: October 28, 2026.

Who this app is for

The app is a private tool operated by RoomwiseTools, a small online shop. It is used only by the shop owner, on the owner's own computer, to publish the shop's own product pins to the shop's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and is not used with anyone else's Pinterest account.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects the app, Pinterest asks the owner to approve access. The app then uses Pinterest's official API to read the owner's boards and to create boards and pins on the owner's own account. The app never sees or stores the owner's Pinterest password.

What is stored, and where

The Pinterest access token, the app ID and the app secret are saved in files on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded, sold or shared. The pin images, titles, descriptions and links the app posts are created by the owner (with AI assistance) and are stored on the same computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information about Pinterest users, viewers, followers or customers.
  • We do not read or store other people's Pinterest data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not post anything the owner has not approved.

AI use

Pin text and images are prepared with the help of AI tools. Pins posted by the app are marked as AI-modified on Pinterest.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in Pinterest under Settings, Apps, or by deleting the saved token file on the computer. Deleting the file removes all stored credentials.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. To contact us, send a message through our Etsy shop: roomwisetools.etsy.com.
